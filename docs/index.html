HealPray Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

No Account Required

HealPray does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Information We Collect

HealPray does not collect personal identifiers such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.

Any prayers, reflections, or notes entered by the user are stored locally on the user’s device only.

Permissions Used

  • Microphone – only when the user chooses to record voice prayers
  • Camera – only when the user chooses to capture content
  • Location – optional, used only to provide contextual spiritual content
  • Notifications – used for prayer reminders

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No health or medical data
  • No heart rate, steps, or sleep data
  • No Health Connect data
  • No Apple Health data
  • No biometric data
  • No advertising identifiers

Data Storage and Retention

All user-generated content remains on the user’s device. HealPray does not maintain user accounts or retain personal data on external servers.

Data Deletion

Users can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s storage via their device settings.
